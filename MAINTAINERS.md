## Contribution Process

1. **Issues** - Anyone can open issues
2. **Pull Requests** - Community contributions welcome
3. **Review** - Core maintainers review PRs
4. **Merge** - Approved PRs merged by maintainers
5. **Release** - Lead maintainer handles releases

## Decision Making

- **Minor changes** - Any core maintainer can approve
- **Major changes** - Consensus among core maintainers
- **Breaking changes** - Community RFC process required

## Becoming a Maintainer

We welcome new maintainers who:

- Contribute high-quality PRs consistently
- Engage constructively with community
- Demonstrate expertise in relevant areas
- Commit to ongoing maintenance

**Process:**
1. Nominated by existing maintainer
2. Consensus among current maintainers
3. 3-month trial period
4. Full maintainer status after successful trial
